# cohort.

> *your person for today.*

Cohort is a purpose-first activity-matching app that connects people around what they want to do — not who they want to meet. No friendship pressure. Just the right person for the right thing, right now.

## The Idea

Most apps ask who you want to meet.
Cohort asks what you want to do.

Built and researched across:

* Jobs-to-be-done research across 12 interviews
* Competitive analysis across 5 products
* Two-sided marketplace strategy
* Cold start problem solving
* North star metrics and full metrics framework
* AI/ML product architecture
* Trust and safety design
* GTM wedge strategy across 3 launch phases
